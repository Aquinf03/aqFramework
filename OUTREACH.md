## 7. Message templates (keep short)

### Guide / roast
> I’m building Aquin — train = directory, recipe = API, live metrics on disk. We’re past toy LMs; real HF/sklearn trains + honest fail-closed knobs. Would you spare 20 minutes to roast the model or tell me what would make this usable in your lab?

### Beta
> Can I send a minimal train folder for your [SFT/LoRA/tabular] setup? You run `aq train` / `aq eval`; I fix whatever breaks. No lock-in — files stay yours.

### Opportunity
> I’m looking for [MTS intern / research eng / RA] focused on training systems + honest tooling. Artifact: [link]. Happy to do a work trial on your stack.

### Design partner (Sarvam-style)
> We want one serious fine-tune partner. You’ll get rapid fixes; we get a real workload. Folder protocol + CLI, not another notebook wrapper.
